# A-SI-v1: selection invariance with unresolved reserves

Approved for implementation and offline validation only. This is not curator
approval, freeze authorization, or authorization for provider requests. The
original protocols and source evidence remain unchanged.

The fixed 24-record A-S1 panel, reference date, four cohorts, seed 0, existing
SHA-256 ranking and two-per-cohort target are preserved. At most two records may
remain pending solely because their publisher/preprint relationship is unresolved.
Their source identifiers and authoritative v1 dates must remain intact and
corroborated. Pending records are never relabeled included or excluded.

All other records require the existing explicit curator decisions and evidence
validation. Evaluate every subset of the pending records as a hypothetical
eligibility completion. Proceed only if every completion yields the identical
ordered eight approved records with no shortfalls. Hypothetical completions are
mathematical sensitivity checks, not identity judgments or eligibility approvals.

A separate curator relationship assessment must bind the exact frame, complete
review fingerprint, pending IDs and proposed eight IDs. It must attest that no
unresolved duplicate/version relationship threatens those eight and explain the
review basis. Recorded possible-duplicate edges, shared identifiers or unresolved
identity claims affecting the selected set must still block selection; an
attestation cannot override those checks. Unknown unrecorded relationships cannot
be ruled out mechanically, and that limit must be disclosed.

The selection certificate retains all 24 dispositions, every completion and its
result, pending IDs/reasons, amendment hash and assessment fingerprint. The pilot
is described as eight verified selected papers with unresolved reserve records,
not as a fully adjudicated panel. Freeze/replay, if separately authorized later,
must retain the original full frame, full review and relationship assessment.

H01 (2610.25566) and H02 (2609.30968) remain pending. Their publisher DOIs are not
equated with their arXiv records. Approval of this amendment does not approve the
other 22 records or any arXiv DOI aliases. Default selection and Experiment B
retain their existing behavior.
