EXPERIMENT A · EXPLORATORY PILOT

Methods & limitations.

01

Dataset

Eight verified arXiv papers, two per fixed first-submission-age cohort (1–7, 8–14, 15–30 and 31–60 days), relative to November 9, 2026 UTC. Papers come from selected cs.CL source days, not a representative sample of all research.

02

Resolution

One title, author and year search per provider and paper; no DOI or arXiv identity hint. Original provider ranking is preserved. The first OpenAlex search was anonymous; the other seven used an API key. Crossref searches were anonymous. All evaluated searches returned HTTP 200. Two earlier OpenAlex 429 responses are retained separately as operational attempts.

03

Identity and metrics

Verified identifiers or provenance-backed mappings support identity. Different identifiers, different titles or similar text alone do not establish identity or distinctness. Recall@5 bounds range from confirmed matches to confirmed plus undetermined matches. No result means the recorded search returned no candidates—not that a paper is missing from a database.

04

Interpretation

This is cross-sectional verified retrieval coverage under the recorded search strategy—not indexing latency. Provider scope and access conditions differ, identities remain unresolved, and eight papers cannot support population-level conclusions or statistical comparisons. No confidence intervals or significance tests are presented.

05

Reproducibility and publication

The export contains selected citation metadata and candidate judgments derived from the offline evaluation. Raw captures and credentials are excluded. This is a draft pending owner security and privacy review. Curator verification and origin labels are supported by retained provenance, not independently guaranteed.
