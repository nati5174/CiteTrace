03 / METHODOLOGY

Designed to leave
room for doubt.

A missing search result is an observation. A returned candidate is a possibility. A verified identity requires evidence that connects the records.

Read the methodology ↗
01

Ground truth first

Eight source-verified arXiv papers, selected from fixed submission-age cohorts.

02

Search without shortcuts

Title, authors and year only. Canonical identifiers are withheld from provider queries.

03

Keep uncertainty visible

Identity judgments use verified identifiers or supported mappings. Similar text alone is not proof.
